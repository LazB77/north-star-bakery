Our Story

North Star Bakery was created as a neighborhood place where customers can find familiar favorites and handmade baked goods. Our goal is to make every visit feel welcoming, whether someone is picking up one loaf or planning treats for a community gathering.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We choose ingredients with quality, consistency, and responsible use in mind. Seasonal availability helps guide our rotating features, while our core recipes stay focused on simple ingredients and careful preparation.

Meet the Team

Our Bakers

Our baking team prepares breads and pastries in small batches and keeps an eye on quality from mixing through the final bake.

Our Front Counter Team

Our customer-facing staff helps neighbors with product questions, pickup planning, and pre-order requests for celebrations and events.
